Classify the editorial content type, not the topic. Return one of model, product, industry, paper, tutorial, opinion.
model: the main news is a newly released, opened, or updated MODEL. Merely using an existing model, discussing its implications, or testing it does not qualify.
product: introduces or updates a usable application, tool, service, SDK, or feature. A product overview, demo, recommendation, or short user endorsement also belongs here, even without a release date. Code snippets and descriptions of features do not by themselves turn a product introduction into a tutorial.
industry: reports a concrete organizational action such as funding, acquisitions, appointments, partnerships, construction, enacted regulation, or scheduled government talks. Opinions expressed by industry figures, forecasts, and analysis of a trend are NOT organizational actions for this taxonomy.
paper: reports a research contribution: a paper, technical report, new method, scientific result, controlled experiment, or discovery. A security investigation that establishes a new vulnerability or capability can be research. Merely mentioning a model in such a result does not make it a model release.
tutorial: teaches, explains, implements, or independently evaluates technology: a guide, engineering breakdown, practical method, reusable skill/prompt, or comparative hands-on benchmark. In an independent review, the product release is background; the reviewer's measurements are the contribution.
opinion: interprets an event or phenomenon, expresses an argument, reports someone's views, forecasts a trend, or discusses implications, risks, and significance. Data and news-style writing can support this content type. A quoted opinion is still opinion. Describing an impressive AI accomplishment mainly to argue about AI's future is opinion rather than a model release.
Identify the article's main contribution rather than classifying its opening background sentence. Where an introduction is followed by substantive independent measurements or engineering explanation, classify that contribution. Conversely, a short reaction to a product announcement does not erase the actual product information present in the input. Do not infer missing linked content.
In reason, briefly state the concrete contribution and the decisive boundary. Use Chinese for the explanation. Check that the chosen enum satisfies its definition: especially do not select model without a model release/update, industry merely for an industry-related topic, or paper merely because the text contains numbers.
